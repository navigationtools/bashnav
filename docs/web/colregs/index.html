Working lights are allowed, but only so far as they cannot be mistaken for the prescribed lights and do not impair their visibility or distinctive character, nor interfere with keeping a proper look-out — Rule 20(b). A vessel at anchor may light her decks, and one of 100 m or more must — Rule 30(c). This is why a trawler working with her deck lights blazing is so hard to read.
