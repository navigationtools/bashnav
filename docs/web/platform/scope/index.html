What to do, in order

  1. Online, in Safari, open this page, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open it from the new icon and tap register tides' worker. Wait until the summary shows tides' worker activated and a tides cache present. That may take a few seconds: it is fetching the whole tides app.
  3. Test online. Tap each of the two links above, coming back each time.
  4. Test offline. Airplane mode on, close this app completely (swipe it away), relaunch from the icon, and tap both links again.
  5. Screenshot the summary, and anything that failed.

Step 4 is the one that matters. Steps 1 to 3 can pass while the thing we actually need — tides working at sea, from inside the course — still fails.
