Reading a QR code

The picture below is a real observation — one nav and one wx record — made by the watch’s own encoder (WeatherGlass/tools/qr_ref.py): byte mode, level L, version 10, mask 0. The question was whether this browser can read it by itself. It cannot — BarcodeDetector is absent on iPadOS and iOS — so deck-log carries a decoder, and the rows below show both answers on this device.

Point it at the picture above on another screen, or at the watch. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is kept.
